## Video 1: Advantage vs Supplement

Length: about 45 seconds. One whiteboard, one marker, Christian on camera.

1. Write two headings: "Original Medicare + Medigap" and "Medicare Advantage."
2. Under Medigap: "Helps pay some costs Original Medicare leaves you. Separate premium. Providers who accept Medicare. Drug plan is separate (Part D)."
3. Under Advantage: "A private plan delivers Part A and Part B. Most include drugs. You still pay Part B. Check the network, approvals, copays, and the yearly limit."
4. Circle three questions, already the decision on the site: which doctors you want to keep, what you pay each month and when you get care, and whether you can still switch later.
5. Write the deadline that does not come back: the six-month Medigap window starts when you are 65 or older and enrolled in Part B. After it, health questions can affect an application.
6. Close on camera: "Neither is better for everyone. Bring your doctors and your prescriptions. The review is free." Show (919) 408-6671 and christianbrinkleync.com/advantage-vs-medigap.

## Video 2: 3 myths about Medicare

Length: about 60 seconds. Cross out each myth, then write the correction.

1. Myth: "One of them is just better." Correction, from the page: the choice depends on doctors, prescriptions, budget, and preferences.
2. Myth: "I can always switch from Advantage to a supplement later." Correction: you may be able to, but acceptance is not guaranteed. Check Medigap eligibility and any health questions before leaving an Advantage plan.
3. Myth: "The dental and vision extras decide it." Correction: some Advantage plans include them. Review the limits, which providers you can use, and the cost, along with the medical and drug coverage.
4. Close: "If you and your spouse want different things, you can choose different plans. Each of you still needs your own review." Same phone number. Point people to the site, not a new landing page.
