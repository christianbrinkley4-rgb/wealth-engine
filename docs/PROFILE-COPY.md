into evidence. Client-facing work under regulatory pressure, plus a shipped

accounting master's candidates have. The AI paragraph is specific about what you
did rather than claiming a skill, which is what makes it credible.

# What else to create, in the order I'd do it

## Do these this week — free, and they compound

1. **Bing Places** — bingplaces.com. Imports from your Google profile in about
   five minutes. ChatGPT's search runs on Bing, so this is the most direct fix
   for assistants not knowing you exist.
2. **Apple Business Connect** — businessconnect.apple.com. Free. Decides what
   Siri and iPhone Maps say about you. A large share of 65-year-olds use
   iPhones, and this is the listing nobody's competitors bother with.
3. **Nextdoor Business Page** — above.
4. **Yelp** — free claim. Ranks well for "insurance agent Greensboro" and is
   read by the assistants. Seniors do check it.

## Worth real money

5. **Greensboro Chamber of Commerce.** A paid membership, and the member
   directory link is one of the few genuinely local, genuinely followed links
   available to you. This is the single best answer to your zero-backlinks
   problem.
6. **Better Business Bureau accreditation.** Costs a few hundred a year. The
   BBB badge carries disproportionate weight with the over-65 demographic, more
   than it does with anyone younger.

## The two highest-leverage things for AI specifically

7. **A YouTube channel.** Four or five phone-shot videos, two minutes each,
   answering one question: "What happens if I miss my Medicare window?" "Can I
   keep my doctor?" YouTube is the second-largest search engine, Google
   surfaces video for these queries, and assistants cite transcripts. It also
   solves your no-social-proof problem — people trust a face.
8. **Reddit, carefully.** r/medicare gets these questions daily, and AI models
   weight Reddit heavily in training and in search. Answering genuinely and
   well, with a profile that says who you are, builds the kind of reputation
   assistants repeat. **Read each subreddit's self-promotion rules first** —
   dropping links gets you banned, and a ban is permanent. Be useful; let people
   click your profile.

## Skip

- Paid "500 directory submission" packages. Google discounts them.
- Insurance lead directories that require handing over prospects. A link is not
  worth contradicting the one promise your whole site makes.
- Twitter/X and Instagram. Your buyers are not there in numbers that justify
  the work, and a dead account looks worse than none.
