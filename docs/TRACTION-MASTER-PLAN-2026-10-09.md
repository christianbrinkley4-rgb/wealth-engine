What the weight is made of [lab]:

- **Google tag**: 169 KB on every page, about 450 to 570 ms of main-thread time.
- **Meta Pixel**: 145 KB in two requests on every page, about 350 to 560 ms, and it sets Meta's `fr` third-party cookie. Paid ads are parked (see memory and MEASUREMENT-SETUP), so this cost currently buys nothing.
- **Fonts**: three preloaded files of 146 KB, 118 KB and 33 KB.
- **Cloudflare Turnstile on every guide page**: about 725 KB in five requests. `GuideCapture` calls `loadTurnstile()` when it mounts ([code] `app/components/GuideCapture.tsx:45`), so every reader of a guide downloads the bot check whether or not they touch the email field.
- **Homepage document**: about 5 s of main-thread time attributed to the page itself, mostly style, layout and rendering. The page is roughly 15,600 px tall on a phone.

The October 5 and October 8 documents scored "page speed feel" at 8 of 10 from a local machine. Under mobile throttling that does not hold. Largest Contentful Paint is 3.5 to 5.7 s in the lab, and Google's "good" threshold is 2.5 s.

## 2. The five decisions that matter most

1. **The root site promises one thing: Medicare help in the Triad from a named local agent.** Money education stays, as a separate lane with its own front door at `/wealth`. The homepage H1 today is "Medicare and money, explained by someone who lives here," and the main nav carries five overlapping education labels. Pick the lane per page and stop blending. Detail in the IA decision.
2. **Fix measurement before building anything else, and decide on the Meta Pixel.** Most useful actions are uncounted, a booking is double-counted, and attended appointments are not recorded anywhere this plan can see. Separately, the Meta Pixel costs about 145 KB and half a second on every page, sets an ad cookie on a Medicare site, and has no campaign to feed. Recommendation: switch it off in Netlify until a Meta campaign is approved. That is Christian's call and an environment change, not code.
3. **Take the 40 retirement and life insurance town pages out of the index, and rewrite or noindex the thin Medicare town pages.** Measured unique content is 1% to 11%. Keep the 11 hand-written town pages.
4. **Link the pages that were built to rank.** The Greensboro Medigap page has no inbound link at all, and the 2027 pages have one or two. This is a small code change with a direct effect on crawl and discovery.
5. **Cut the weight every page carries.** Lazy-load Turnstile, load the Google tag after the page is usable, and trim fonts. Lab LCP is 3.5 to 5.7 s on a phone profile.
