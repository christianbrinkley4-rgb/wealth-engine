## Niche snapshot

Christian Brinkley is a licensed NC Life & Health insurance agent in Greensboro, working the Medicare lane: turning-65 education, Annual Enrollment Period plan reviews, Medigap vs Medicare Advantage comparisons, and Part D guidance. Audience is seniors 64-70 in the Piedmont Triad (Greensboro, Winston-Salem, High Point, Burlington) plus Granville County. His site already has strong citable assets: 2027 Medicare cost figures, a Part D donut-hole explainer, and city-specific pages. Everything below is verified real (confirmed via web search snippets or page fetches on the research date). No paid-link schemes, no spam directories.

## Competitor backlink patterns

I searched for pages linking to **moserinsurancegroup.com** and **medicareboutique.com** (his two named Greensboro competitors). Finding: no deep editorial or resource-page backlinks surfaced for either one. Their visible footprint is almost entirely aggregator and citation-level: Google Business Profile, Yelp, Facebook, and directory listings. Neither appears to have local editorial links (news, senior centers, libraries, churches), guest articles, or citable data assets pointing at them.

That is the opening. Both competitors are citation-heavy and editorial-light. If Christian earns even a handful of local editorial and resource-page links (senior centers, UNC-curated health info, Triad press), he out-links them where it counts for both Google and AI-search citations. A full backlink inventory would need an SEO tool like Ahrefs or Moz; the pattern above is what public search shows.

## Top 5 picks

1. **NC Health Info (row 23).** UNC-Chapel Hill librarian-curated, NC-specific, already links Medicare resources. His 2027 cost pages are exactly what they collect. High authority, and AI search engines cite pages like this.
2. **AAMSI directory activation (row 1).** The invite is already accepted. One step from live. The niche association's own directory is the most topically relevant link available.
3. **Senior Resources of Guilford / Evergreens (row 8).** Biggest senior organization in his home county, 90+ monthly programs, certified Senior Center of Excellence. A workshop there means a local link plus real in-person leads.
4. **Medicare Agents Hub expansion (row 2).** He is already listed in Greensboro. Getting onto the Winston-Salem, High Point, and Burlington pages multiplies the backlinks from a site that already ranks for "medicare agents near me" queries.
5. **MyMedicareBroker.com free tier (row 3).** Launched August 2026 with a permanently free tier and NIPR-verified listings. Claiming an early profile on a new directory is cheap now and compounds as the directory grows.
