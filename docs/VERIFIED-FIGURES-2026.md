# Verified 2026 Figures — use these, nothing else

Checked October 8, 2026. Every figure below was verified against IRS.gov,
SSA, or another primary source on that date. Copy these numbers exactly.
If a page needs a figure not on this list, verify it with a web search
against a primary source first. Never invent a number.

## Federal income tax brackets, 2026 (IRS Rev. Proc. 2025-32, IR-2025-103)

Rates: 10%, 12%, 22%, 24%, 32%, 35%, 37%. Applied to taxable income.

Single:
- 10%: $0 to $12,400
- 12%: $12,401 to $50,400
- 22%: $50,401 to $105,700
- 24%: $105,701 to $201,775
- 32%: $201,776 to $256,225
- 35%: $256,226 to $640,600
- 37%: over $640,600

Married filing jointly:
- 10%: $0 to $24,800
- 12%: $24,801 to $100,800
- 22%: $100,801 to $211,400
- 24%: $211,401 to $403,550
- 32%: $403,551 to $512,450
- 35%: $512,451 to $768,700
- 37%: over $768,700

## Standard deduction, 2026 (IRS IR-2025-103)

- Single: $16,100
- Married filing jointly: $32,200
- Head of household: $24,150
- Additional for age 65+ or blind: $2,050 (single or head of household),
  $1,650 per qualifying spouse (married)

## North Carolina income tax, 2026

- Flat 3.99% individual income tax rate (Tax Foundation, 2026 state tax data)

## Payroll taxes, 2026

- Social Security wage base: $184,500 (SSA)
- Social Security tax: 6.2% employee / 6.2% employer, up to the wage base
- Medicare tax: 1.45% employee / 1.45% employer, no wage cap
- Additional Medicare tax: 0.9% on wages over $200,000 single / $250,000 joint
  (employee only)

## 401(k) / 403(b) / governmental 457(b), 2026 (IRS Notice 2025-67)

- Employee deferral limit: $24,500
- Catch-up, age 50+: $8,000 (total $32,500)
- Super catch-up, ages 60-63: $11,250 (total $35,750)
- Combined employee + employer limit: $72,000
- New for 2026: catch-up contributions must be Roth if prior-year wages
  exceeded $150,000

## IRA (traditional + Roth combined), 2026 (IRS)

- Under 50: $7,500
- Age 50+: $8,600 ($7,500 + $1,100 catch-up)
- Roth IRA phase-out, single: $153,000 to $168,000 MAGI
- Roth IRA phase-out, married filing jointly: $242,000 to $252,000 MAGI

## HSA, 2026 (IRS Rev. Proc. 2025-19)

- Self-only coverage: $4,400
- Family coverage: $8,750
- Catch-up, age 55+: $1,000
- HDHP minimum deductible: $1,700 self-only / $3,400 family
- HDHP out-of-pocket max: $8,500 self-only / $17,000 family
- Employer contributions count toward the limit

## Medicare, 2027 (CMS, already on the site)

- Part D max deductible: $700
- Part D out-of-pocket cap: $2,400
- Medicare Advantage average premium: about $12/month (CMS projection)

## North Carolina 529 plan

- No state income tax deduction for contributions (eliminated 2014)
- Growth is tax-deferred; qualified withdrawals are tax-free for federal
  and NC purposes

## Retirement ages (federal law)

- RMDs begin at age 73 (SECURE 2.0)
- Social Security full retirement age: 67 for anyone born 1960 or later
