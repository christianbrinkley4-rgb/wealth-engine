## First conversion change

- The home page's first button opens a short Medicare contact form. The free enrollment date tool remains linked beside it.
- A second path offers a coverage review to people already on Medicare before the October 15–December 7 open enrollment period. [Medicare confirms those dates](https://www.medicare.gov/health-drug-plans/open-enrollment).
- The short path puts name, email, optional phone, and ZIP before optional meeting details. Required consent and the bot check remain in place. The original question flow is still available.
- Short path inquiries record `entry_mode: quick_contact` so their outcome can be compared with the older flow. No private answers go to analytics pixels.
- Christian confirmed **(919) 408-6671** as the website and social contact number and confirmed **8 organizations and 65 products** for the current Medicare disclaimer on October 1.
- CMS changed the standardized TPMO disclaimer for 2027 marketing materials starting October 1, 2026. The site uses the [new wording](https://www.cms.gov/files/document/changes-medicare-advantage-organization-medicare-drug-plan-sponsors-marketing-guidelines.pdf).
