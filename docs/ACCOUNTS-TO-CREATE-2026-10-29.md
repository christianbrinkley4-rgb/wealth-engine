## Why "near me" searches work differently

For "medicare agent near me", "turning 65 help near me" and "life insurance near me", Google shows a map with three businesses above the regular results. Who gets those three spots depends on:

1. **How close the searcher is.** You can't change this. Your Greensboro profile will show for people in and around Greensboro. The city pages on the site are how you reach people in Winston-Salem, High Point and Kernersville.
2. **How well the profile matches the search.** Category, services, description and posts.
3. **How trusted the business looks.** Mostly the count and freshness of reviews, plus matching listings across the web. That's what the rest of this list builds.

Below the map, the site already shows up for some of these. Over the last 90 days "medicare agent near me" averaged position 6 and "medicare specialist near me" 5.2, though only on a handful of searches each. Getting those to the top 3 takes local trust signals, not more pages.
