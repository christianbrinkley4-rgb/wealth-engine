# Traffic mission research and proposed batch

Research date: October 8, 2026. The requested keyword battle plan was missing from the fetched production branch. The existing traffic playbook was read before selection.

## Search observations

Queries searched included overtime deduction 2026 married filing separately; dependent teen summer-job taxes; 1099-K personal items at a loss; extension unable to pay; unemployment withholding; inherited IRA annual withdrawals; HSA/FSA and job changes; 401(k) rollover checks; Greensboro Medicare nonrenewal 2027.

- Overtime and tips: IRS guidance is prominent. Opportunity is precise pay classification and records, not lack of authoritative answers.
- Teens: results mix tax years and earned-income thresholds. Separate dependent income tests from self-employment.
- 1099-K and unpaid taxes: IRS answers exist; lead with a practical records workflow.
- Inherited IRAs: IRS Publication 590-B and national publishers compete. Avoid the misleading idea that every beneficiary may wait ten years.
- Job changes: large retirement providers have strong coverage. Differentiate the check-payee issue and HSA/FSA comparison rather than suggesting broad ranking dominance.
- Local Medicare: broad enrollment content surfaced. Local unmet demand is a hypothesis; no carrier exit was verified or asserted.
