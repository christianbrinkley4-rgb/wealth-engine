What no competitor can copy

A named local agent who publishes the county rules, tells people when the answer is “do nothing”, and picks up his own phone. eHealth cannot be local. A local agent will not build these tools. That intersection is the whole business.

What decides it anyway

Three Google reviews, a real domain, and an email provider that actually sends. None of them are design problems, and all three outrank every remaining polish item on this page.
